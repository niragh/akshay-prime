Physical Styles of Hearing Aids

BTEBehind-The-Ear

The most powerful, durable design with large tactile buttons. Great for all hearing loss levels.

RICReceiver-In-Canal

Ultra-discreet, open-fit natural sound with direct Bluetooth streaming and rechargeable batteries.

ITEIn-The-Ear

100% Invisible from face-to-face angles. Engineered past the second ear canal bend for maximum discretion.
