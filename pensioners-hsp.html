Commonwealth Hearing Services Program (HSP)

Government-Funded Hearing Care for Pensioners & Veterans

Prime Audiology is an accredited provider under the Australian Government Hearing Services Program. We help eligible Australians receive fully-funded hearing care with zero paperwork hassle.

Eligibility Criteria

Who Qualifies for Fully-Funded Support?

If you are an Australian citizen or permanent resident, you are eligible for the Commonwealth Hearing Services Program (HSP) if you hold any of the following:

✓

Centrelink Pensioner Concession Card

Includes Age Pension, Disability Support Pension, or Carer Payment recipients.
✓

Department of Veterans' Affairs (DVA) Gold or White Card

White Card specific for hearing conditions, or any Gold Card holder.
✓

Australian Defence Force (ADF) Membership

Current serving members or referred personnel.
✓

Dependent of a Concession Card Holder

Spouse or dependent of an eligible pensioner.

All Funding Pathways

Additional Hearing Support & Rebates

Beyond the Commonwealth HSP, multiple Australian funding schemes can significantly reduce or eliminate your hearing care costs.

Medicare Rebates

If your GP provides a referral under a Chronic Disease Management (CDM) plan or from an ENT specialist / Neurologist, you can claim a Medicare rebate for diagnostic audiology assessments.

National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS)

For Australians under 65 with permanent sensorineural hearing loss affecting daily communication and autonomy. We assist with assistive technology (AT) quotes and reports.

My Aged Care (Home Care Packages)

If you have a Level 1–4 Home Care Package, unallocated package funds can be utilized to cover mobile home visits, hearing device accessories, and specialized maintenance.

Private Health Insurance (HICAPS)

Depending on your extras policy, health funds (Bupa, Medibank, HCF, NIB, etc.) provide rebates of up to $1,000+ per device every 3 to 5 years. We provide instant HICAPS claiming on-site.

WorkCover (Noise-Induced Loss)

If your hearing was damaged through industrial or workplace noise exposure, WorkSafe may fully cover hearing testing, premium hearing aids, and lifelong battery replacements.

Self-Funded Patients

Transparent, bundle-free options starting from $1,600/pair. We provide itemized quotes with zero hidden ongoing fees and a full 30-day trial period.
