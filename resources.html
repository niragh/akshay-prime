How Hearing Aids Work: Sound Production to Brain

We hear with our brains, not merely our ears. When sound enters the ear canal, microscopic hair cells in the cochlea convert mechanical acoustic vibrations into neural electrical impulses transmitted along the auditory nerve to the brain’s temporal cortex.

Modern digital hearing aids don't just amplify sound—they preserve acoustic contrasts, reduce noise, and protect cognitive vitality.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about consultations, trial periods, and government assistance.

Do I need a doctor's referral to see George Sebastian? +
No referral is required for private or pensioner audiological appointments. You can book directly with us anytime. If you wish to claim a Medicare rebate via a GP Chronic Disease Management plan, a GP referral letter is necessary.
How does your 30-day hearing aid trial work? +
Following your comprehensive evaluation and Real-Ear verification fitting, you take the hearing aids home for 30 days. You test them in your actual daily life—at home with family, watching TV, and in restaurants. We schedule follow-up tuning appointments with George to ensure perfect comfort before any final commitment.
Does micro-suction ear wax removal hurt? +
Not at all. Micro-suction is a gentle procedure. You will hear a rushing wind sound as the suction tube operates, but it is completely dry and safe. Because George watches through a high-magnification surgical microscope, your delicate ear canal lining is protected at all times.
Can I get rechargeable hearing aids through the Pensioner HSP Scheme? +
Yes. The Government Hearing Services Program provides a set baseline subsidy that covers fully-funded standard digital hearing aids. If you prefer rechargeable lithium-ion models or advanced Bluetooth features, you can opt for a "Top-Up" device where the Government subsidy is applied directly, and you simply pay the remaining difference.
How do I arrange a home visit consultation? +
Simply call our clinic at (03) 8845 7440 or choose "Home Visit Request" in our online appointment form. George Sebastian coordinates directly with you or your family to arrange a convenient day and time.
