Device Anatomy

Hearing Aid Styles & Physical Form Factors

Explore the physical options available. During your appointment, George will inspect your ear canal shape and recommend the most comfortable design.

BTE Behind-The-Ear
  • Highest amplification power
  • Easiest for seniors with arthritis or dexterity needs
  • Long battery life & robust moisture resistance
RIC Receiver-In-Canal
  • Australia's most popular modern choice
  • Natural open ear feeling without occlusion
  • Direct Bluetooth streaming & overnight charging
ITE In-The-Ear
  • Custom-moulded to the exact ear bowl
  • Never interferes with spectacles or sunglasses
  • Simple one-piece insertion
ITC In-The-Canal
  • Sits in lower ear canal opening
  • Subtle cosmetic appearance
  • Utilises ear pinna for natural acoustics
CIC Completely-In-Canal
  • Substantially hidden from face-to-face view
  • Low wind sensitivity for outdoor walking
  • Discreet removal cord
IIC Invisible-In-Canal
  • 100% Invisible from normal conversation angles
  • Rests deep past second ear canal bend
  • Ultimate cosmetic discretion
