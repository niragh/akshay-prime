Visiting Us

Clinic Accessibility & Location Details

Designed from the ground up for easy, stress-free access for older patients and family drivers.

Dedicated Patient Parking

Reserved street-level patient parking located right outside the clinic entrance. No multi-level parking ramps or long walks required.

Step-Free Wheelchair Access

Completely level, step-free access throughout the building, waiting lounge, and audiological testing suites to accommodate walking frames and wheelchairs.

Home Visits Available

If traveling to the clinic is not possible, our mobile team brings full diagnostic sound testing directly into your home anywhere in Melbourne.

Principal Audiologist: George Sebastian (M.Aud, MAudA CCP). Dedicated to unhurried, patient-first hearing healthcare.
